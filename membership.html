Join HUDI

Become a member and help shape the direction of HUDI's work in Kalobeyei Settlement.

Membership

What membership means

Who Can Become a Member

Membership is open to persons with disabilities, parents and caregivers, community volunteers and any resident of Kalobeyei Settlement or the host community who supports HUDI's mission of disability inclusion, aged 18 and above.

Membership Benefits

  • A vote at the General Assembly and a say in HUDI's direction
  • Priority information on programmes, trainings and events
  • Eligibility to stand for election to the Management Committee
  • Being part of a recognised, organised advocacy voice

Responsibilities

  • Uphold and promote HUDI's constitution and values
  • Attend the General Assembly where possible
  • Represent HUDI positively in the community
  • Keep membership records (contact details, status) up to date

Requirements

  • Be 18 years or older
  • Reside in Kalobeyei Settlement or the surrounding host community
  • Complete and submit the application form below
  • Agree to HUDI's constitution and code of conduct

Membership Fee Information

HUDI keeps membership accessible so that cost is never a barrier to joining. Current registration and annual fees are confirmed by the Management Committee and shared with applicants once your application is reviewed — contact our office if you'd like the current fee schedule in advance.
