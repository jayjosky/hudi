What is happening at HUDI

Programme milestones, community events and stories from Kalobeyei Settlement — updated as our work continues.

Training·18 June 2026

Teachers across Kalobeyei complete inclusive education training

Thirty-two teachers from primary schools in Kalobeyei completed a three-day workshop on adapting lessons, classroom layout and assessment for learners with disabilities.

Full coverage of this story, including photos and community voices, will be published here as our newsroom grows. Contact the HUDI office if you would like more detail or would like to feature this work.

Assistive devices reach 40 households in Village 2

Working with partners, HUDI distributed wheelchairs, crutches, walking frames and hearing aids, followed by home visits to make sure each device fits well.

Community dialogue on disability rights draws elders and youth

Community leaders, parents and young people met to discuss stigma, access to services and the practical steps that make public spaces usable for everyone.
