About HUDI

A community-based organisation led by and for our neighbours

Hands Up for Disability Inclusion is based in Kalobeyei Settlement, Turkana West. We work with refugee and host communities so that persons with disabilities are identified early, supported practically, and included in every part of community life.

  • Community-based rehabilitation delivered at home and in the neighbourhood.
  • Inclusive education support for learners, teachers and parents.
  • Advocacy that puts persons with disabilities at the centre of decisions.

Success Story

“With my wheelchair, I can leave my shelter and go where I need to go”

She was identified during a HUDI mobility screening in the settlement, where restricted movement was keeping her from school and community life. Our team fitted her with a properly sized wheelchair and trained her family on its use and care, and a HUDI outreach worker continues to check in on her progress at home.

HUDI Outreach Team — Kalobeyei Settlement
