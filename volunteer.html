Volunteer With Us

Give your time and skills to outreach, therapy support and training days in Kalobeyei Settlement.

Why Volunteer

Your time makes a direct difference

Volunteers extend the reach of our small team into every village of the settlement, supporting families who would otherwise wait far longer for help.

Why Volunteer

Volunteering with HUDI puts your time directly into the hands of children and adults with disabilities who need support the most. You'll work alongside experienced rehabilitation workers, educators and community mobilisers, gaining hands-on experience in disability-inclusive development while helping close real gaps in care.

Volunteer Opportunities

  • Community outreach and household follow-up visits
  • Assisting rehabilitation and therapy sessions
  • Supporting inclusive education in schools
  • Event, training and workshop logistics
  • Data collection, translation and administrative support

Volunteer Expectations

  • Commit to the schedule you agree with your team lead
  • Treat all persons served with dignity and confidentiality
  • Follow HUDI's child and vulnerable-person safeguarding guidelines
  • Attend a short orientation before your first placement

Volunteer Benefits

  • Hands-on experience in community-based rehabilitation
  • A reference letter after satisfactory service
  • Skills training and mentorship from HUDI staff
  • Transport or airtime support for some roles, where available
