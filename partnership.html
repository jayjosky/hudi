Collaboration

Ways to work with us

HUDI works alongside UN agencies, NGOs, government departments, faith-based groups, academic institutions and the private sector. Partnerships can take several forms:

Programmatic Collaboration

Joint delivery or referral pathways across rehabilitation, education, livelihoods or advocacy activities.

Funding & Grants

Project or core funding support that helps HUDI reach more households across the settlement.

In-Kind Support

Assistive devices, training materials, venue space, transport or other resources donated in kind.

Technical Assistance

Capacity building, staff training, or specialist expertise in rehabilitation, education or M&E.

Research & Data

Joint studies, needs assessments or data-sharing that strengthen evidence for disability-inclusive programming.

Referral Network

Two-way referrals so people reach the right specialist services quickly, wherever they first seek help.
