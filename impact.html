Our first year, by the numbers

Success Stories

Behind every number is a person

A few of the changes our members and beneficiaries have described to us.

From the back of the class to the front

A learner with low vision received large-print materials and a seat near the board. Her teacher was coached on inclusive lesson planning, and she now sits national assessments with her peers.

A workshop of her own

After tailoring training and a start-up kit, a young woman with a physical disability now takes orders from three villages and trains two apprentices.

Early support changes everything

A toddler identified during a screening day began therapy at eight months. Today he walks with a frame and attends early childhood classes with his cousins.

Community Impact

Change that outlives a single project

Our work is measured in participation, confidence and access — not only in activities delivered.

Households reached

Regular home visits mean support arrives where families live, not only where services are located.

Attitudes shifting

Community dialogues with elders, youth and faith leaders are reducing stigma and hidden disability.

Systems strengthened

Trained teachers, health workers and volunteers keep inclusion going long after a project ends.

Future Growth

What we are building next

Our priorities for the coming period, shaped by members and community feedback.

Partner with us on these goals
  • Expand early identification screening to every village in Kalobeyei.
  • Establish a dedicated assistive device repair and fitting workshop.
  • Grow parent support groups into savings and livelihood cooperatives.
  • Build a simple data system to track outcomes for every beneficiary.
  • Formalise referral agreements with health and education partners.

Updated impact figures, annual reports and programme evaluations will be published on this page as they are completed.
