Give Today

Your donation funds assistive devices, school support, rehabilitation visits and outreach across Kalobeyei Settlement.

Where Your Support Goes

Choose a cause to support

Every contribution — whatever the size — goes directly toward one of the programme areas below.

1

Support Inclusive Education

Helps children with disabilities learn alongside their peers through teacher training, adapted materials and classroom support.

2

Support Community Rehabilitation

Funds home-based therapy, follow-up visits and rehabilitation support delivered by trained community workers.

3

Support Assistive Devices

Provides wheelchairs, crutches, walking frames, hearing aids and other devices that restore independence.

4

Support Economic Empowerment

Backs livelihood training and small-scale income opportunities for persons with disabilities and their families.

5

Support Community Outreach

Reaches families in every village with household visits, referrals and disability-rights awareness.
