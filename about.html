Who we are and what drives our work

Who we are and what drives our work

HUDI is a community-based organisation in Kalobeyei Settlement, Turkana West, advocating for equal opportunities, rehabilitation, inclusive education and disability rights.

Our Story

Started by the families who needed it most

HUDI grew out of everyday conversations in Kalobeyei about dignity, access and opportunity.

1

Beginnings

A group of parents and persons with disabilities in Kalobeyei began meeting to discuss the barriers their families faced — missed school, missed services, and being left out of community decisions.

2

Organising

The group formalised as a community-based organisation, agreed a constitution, and elected a management committee drawn from both refugee and host communities.

3

Programmes

HUDI launched community-based rehabilitation, early identification screening, inclusive education support and assistive device referrals across the settlement villages.

4

Today

We work with schools, health facilities, humanitarian partners and county departments to make inclusion normal practice — while remaining led by the people it serves.

Who We Are

A community-based organisation led by and for our neighbours

Hands Up for Disability Inclusion (HUDI) is a CBO based in Kalobeyei Settlement, Turkana West. We advocate for equal opportunities, early identification and intervention, community-based rehabilitation, inclusive education and disability rights.

Our Mission

Promote disability inclusion through advocacy, early identification, community-based rehabilitation, capacity building, and equal opportunities.

Our Vision

An inclusive society where persons with disabilities actively participate in all aspects of community life.

Organisational Structure

How decisions are made

A simple, accountable structure that keeps members in charge of the organisation.

1. General Assembly

All registered members. Elects leaders, approves the constitution and sets strategic direction.

2. Management Committee

Chairperson, Secretary, Treasurer and elected members. Oversees governance and accountability.

3. Programme Teams

Rehabilitation, education, advocacy and livelihoods teams delivering day-to-day activities.

4. Community Volunteers

Trained volunteers and parent groups supporting outreach in each village.
